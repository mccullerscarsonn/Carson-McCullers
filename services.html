Services

Tools Within a Larger Strategy.

These are not a menu of automatic recommendations. They are tools within a larger strategy. The right combination depends on what the research reveals about your specific book and situation.

01

Discovery

Making a book findable where readers are already searching.

What It Is

Amazon and platform discoverability

Keyword research, category selection, Amazon SEO, book description optimization, and Goodreads strategy.

Why It Matters

Most readers search, they don't browse

If a book isn't optimized for the way readers actually search and filter, it can remain invisible regardless of quality.

When It's Useful

When visibility lags activity

Often relevant when marketing is happening but on-platform discovery still isn't reflecting it.

02

Research

Understanding the author, the catalog and the market before anything else.

What It Is

Author, catalog and market research

Author research, catalog audits, competitive research, audience research and market analysis.

Why It Matters

Strategy without research is a guess

Recommendations made without understanding the specific situation tend to default to generic tactics.

When It's Useful

Before any new investment

Especially valuable before committing budget or time to a new marketing direction.

03

Positioning

Making sure the book is presented the way its actual readers think about it.

What It Is

Book, author and catalog positioning

Positioning review, website analysis, and social presence analysis.

Why It Matters

Positioning shapes first impressions

A mismatch between how a book is framed and how readers actually categorize it quietly limits discovery.

When It's Useful

When comparisons feel off

Useful when a book is being compared to the wrong titles, or not being compared to anything at all.

04

Promotion

Turning strategy into visible, reader-facing activity.

What It Is

Reader-facing marketing activity

Book trailers, mockups, email marketing, BookTok strategy, content strategy, ARC/review strategy and personalized outreach.

Why It Matters

Execution still matters

Research identifies the opportunity. Promotion is how that opportunity reaches real readers.

When It's Useful

Once priorities are clear

Most effective once research has identified which channels are actually worth the effort.

05

Strategy

Bringing research, positioning and promotion together into a plan.

What It Is

Ongoing marketing strategy

Marketing strategy, campaign planning, discovery strategy, reader acquisition and ongoing measurement.

Why It Matters

Strategy should follow evidence

A plan built on research responds to what the book actually needs, not a generic author to-do list.

When It's Useful

For longer-term direction

Particularly relevant for authors with a catalog, a series, or a long publishing horizon.

"I don't start with a service menu. I start by researching the book. The services above are simply the tools available once that research points somewhere specific."
