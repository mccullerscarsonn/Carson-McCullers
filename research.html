Research

What I Look At Before I Recommend Anything.

Every engagement begins with the same disciplined process: understanding the author, the book, the catalog, the audience and the market before any strategy is proposed.

Research Categories

Eight Areas, Examined Before Anything Is Proposed.

01

Author

Background, platform, voice, and prior marketing history.

02

Book

Positioning, description, categories, keywords and presentation.

03

Catalog

How individual titles relate to the author's full body of work.

04

Audience

Who is actually reading, reviewing and engaging with comparable books.

05

Platform

Amazon, Goodreads, website and social presence as they exist today.

06

Market

Comparable titles, authors and the current competitive landscape.

07

Competition

How similar books are positioned, marketed and discovered.

08

Discovery

Where readers currently find the book, and where they don't.
